About AIstudy

AIstudy is a free study assistant that turns any course material into notes, flashcards, quizzes, practice tests and games.

What AIstudy does

Students spend hours summarizing lectures and making revision material by hand. AIstudy automates the mechanical part of that work so you can spend your time actually studying. Upload a document or paste a link, and AIstudy reads it, extracts the important ideas, and produces study material in a consistent, well-organized format.

AI study notes

Upload PDFs, DOCX files, slide decks or plain text, or paste a link to an article or YouTube video. AIstudy produces clean, structured notes with headings, bullet points and key terms.

Smart flashcards

Flashcards are generated automatically from your material and scheduled with a spaced-repetition system that adapts to how well you remember each card.

Quizzes & practice tests

Generate multiple-choice, true/false and essay questions with explanations for every answer. Practice tests are timed and scored like the real thing.

Study games

Turn your flashcards into arcade-style games — hangman, asteroids, matching and more — so a quick break is still revision.

How it works

  1. Import your material. Drag in a file or paste a link. Everything runs in your browser.
  2. Generate study material. One click creates notes, flashcards and quizzes from the source.
  3. Study your way. Review notes, drill flashcards, take practice tests, or play a game — all from the same material.
  4. Track your mastery. Spaced repetition and quiz scores show you what you know and what needs another pass.

Pricing

AIstudy is free. There are no paywalls, no limits on how much you can generate, and no credit card required. The service is supported by unobtrusive advertising shown alongside content you create, and we never sell your data.

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of files can I import?

PDF documents, Word documents (.docx), plain text, slide decks, article URLs and YouTube links are all supported.

Do I need an account?

No. AIstudy works completely in your browser with nothing to install. An optional free account syncs your notes across devices.

Is my material private?

Your documents are processed to generate study material and are never sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

What is spaced repetition?

Spaced repetition schedules each flashcard for review just before you would normally forget it, which is the most efficient way to build long-term memory. AIstudy uses the FSRS scheduling algorithm.

Which subjects does it work for?

AIstudy is subject-agnostic. It works with anything text-based: medicine, law, languages, engineering, history and more. It performs best with course material that already explains concepts clearly.

Is the project open source?

Yes. The source code is published under the GNU AGPL-3.0 license.
