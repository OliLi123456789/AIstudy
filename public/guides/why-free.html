Why Is AIstudy Free?

A study tool that generates notes, flashcards and quizzes with AI — for $0. Here's how that works, and the honest trade-off that makes it possible.

"What's the catch?" is a fair question. AI is not free to run: every note, flashcard deck and quiz costs a small amount of real compute. Most tools pass that cost to you as a subscription. AIstudy chose a different model: advertising pays for the AI, so your wallet doesn't have to.

The honest trade-off

You see ads on some screens while you study. That's the entire deal. There is no paywall, no credit card, no limit on how many documents you can import, and no "pro" tier hiding the good features. The ads are the price — and we think it's a better deal than a $15/month subscription that most students can't afford.

How the money flows

What we do NOT do with your data

  • We do not sell your notes, study material, or email address.
  • Your documents are sent to the AI provider only to generate your study material, and are not used to train models.
  • We do not show ads based on the content of your study material.

Our Privacy Policy covers this in full, in plain language.

How the AI costs stay low enough for ads to cover

Every request to an AI model costs money, but we keep those costs down in three ways. First, we use caching: when similar material is processed, reused context costs almost nothing. Second, we batch work — generating notes, flashcards and a quiz together is cheaper than three separate calls. Third, we cap what one request can consume, which keeps the service fair for everyone. Small savings per request are the difference between "free with ads" and "not viable."

Could it ever stop being free?

Our commitment is that the core experience — importing material and generating notes, flashcards and quizzes — stays free. If costs ever outgrow advertising, we would add optional paid extras rather than put the existing features behind a paywall. What's free now stays free.

💡 You can help keep it free just by using the product — every page view funds a small slice of someone's study session, yours included.
