Study Guides · Getting Started

From PDF to Exam-Ready in 10 Minutes

AIstudy turns any document into a complete study kit. Here's the exact workflow, start to finish.

Step 1: Import your material

From the dashboard, choose Upload Document or Website Link. Supported inputs include PDFs, Word documents, plain text, slide decks, article URLs and YouTube links. Drag-and-drop works too. Everything runs in your browser; you don't need an account.

Step 2: Generate the study kit

AIstudy reads the source and produces, in one pass:

  • Structured notes — headings, bullet points, and key terms, organized like a clean textbook page.
  • Flashcards — automatically paired question-answer cards, scheduled with spaced repetition.
  • A quiz — multiple-choice, true/false, and fill-in questions with explanations for every answer.
⚡ Generation usually takes well under a minute for a typical lecture or chapter. Longer sources are chunked automatically.

Step 3: Study

  • Read the notes first — get the big picture before drilling details.
  • Flip the flashcards every day. Answer aloud before you reveal the back.
  • Take the quiz to find gaps; every wrong answer comes with an explanation.
  • Play a study game — hangman, matching and arcade-style games built from your cards, for short sessions and breaks.
  • Run a practice test when the exam is near — timed, mixed-difficulty, exam-style.

Step 4: Track mastery

The progress view shows which cards you've mastered and which need another pass, and the quiz history reveals weak topics. Spend your next study session on exactly those weak spots instead of rereading everything.

Tips for the best results

  • Import complete material. A full chapter produces far better notes than a fragment.
  • Study actively. The tools work best when you attempt answers from memory first.
  • Review within 24 hours. The first review matters most — see the forgetting curve guide.
  • Keep sessions short. 20 focused minutes beat two hours of half-attention.
