Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This policy explains what information AIstudy ("we", "our") collects when you use the AIstudy web application (the "Service"), how that information is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Study material you provide. When you import a document or link, the text content is processed to generate study material (notes, flashcards, quizzes).
  • Your notes and study data. Notes, flashcards, quiz results and settings you create are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device.
  • Account information (optional). If you create an account, we store your email address and a password hash. Account data and synced study data are stored with our database provider, Supabase.
  • Technical data. Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps) are collected by our hosting provider to operate and secure the Service.

2. How we use information

  • To generate study material from content you import.
  • To sync your study data across devices when you have an account.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the Service, and to prevent abuse.

We do not sell or rent your personal information or study data to third parties.

3. AI processing

Content you import is sent to our AI provider, DeepSeek, solely to generate study material. The requests are made through our own server-side proxy, and your data is not used by the AI provider to train models or for any purpose other than processing your request. You should not upload material you do not have the right to process, or highly sensitive personal data, to the Service.

4. Advertising

The Service displays advertising provided by Google AdSense to keep the Service free. Google and its partners may use cookies and device identifiers to serve and measure ads. These cookies allow Google to show ads based on visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or by disabling cookies in your browser.

5. Data retention

Notes that have not been opened for 30 days are automatically deleted from browser storage to keep it clean. Account data is retained until you delete your account, which you can do at any time from the Settings screen. Contact us to request deletion of all data associated with your email address.

6. Cookies and local storage

The Service uses browser local storage to keep your notes and preferences on your device. Advertising partners may set cookies as described above. You can clear both at any time through your browser settings.

7. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
